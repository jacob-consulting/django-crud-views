## Retired coverage audit

The removed tests exclusively certified protected destinations: candidate URL syntax, synthetic request binding,
destination permissions/state/queryset/native identity, destination Guardian topology and adapter query budgets.
Their behavior is deliberately removed, rather than replaced by a second authorization framework. Both shared
fixture names/imports remain: read-only inspection, original-actor real detail requests, and component probes use
them. Core custom real-request 403 checks, UUID cascade navigation, source-state evaluation, provider/no-write
GET/POST, preview visibility, escaping/body overrides and actual Guardian source-filter tests remain.

The database suite retains selected-alias protection, pinned atomic/commit routing, per-object/global/group
source projection, cross-alias collision/topology rejection, interleaved actors, and native write safeguards.
Guardian composition and Cancel production helpers are unchanged; their independent request suites remain.
The protected body also retains its non-null URL branch, independently tested for escaping, although stock
context always supplies null URLs. No production template was changed by Plan 01.

## Scope and limitations

Inspection and effective filtering remain complete and may query/materialize the full graph. No constant total
query, bounded graph-memory, full supported-runtime matrix, browser or external-theme claim follows from these
fixtures. Application labels and cascade URL overrides may themselves query. Plan 02 owns bounded selection and
selected-cascade query measurements; this plan establishes removal of protected destination work.
