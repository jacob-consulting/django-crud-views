## Shared Patterns

- **Authorization:** unfiltered blocking first, effective core/Guardian visibility second, display selection last. No destination simulation or new permission resolver.
- **Validation:** raw Any settings plus strict genuine-int predicate, per-view CheckExpression, runtime ImproperlyConfigured. None means unlimited; identity sentinel means inherit.
- **Error handling:** retain transaction/native-delete/custom protection lifecycle and modal 422 response (`delete.py:464-499`). Do not catch new broad exceptions around URL hooks or labels.
- **Presentation:** ordinary Django escaping, i18n and cv_alert body-template seams. One truncation notice reports visible overflow without omitted counts; summaries retain existing redaction policy.
- **Testing:** reuse pytest fixtures/monkeypatch, exact structured context checks, lxml assertions, multi-alias query capture and byte-identical recipe fixtures. Broad verification stays multicore per project instructions.
