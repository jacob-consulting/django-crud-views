## Context and selection

- `has_protected_objects`: always-present, complete unfiltered blocking boolean. Hidden/beyond-cap blockers
  still prevent deletion and omit the whole confirmation form, including its Cancel control.
- `delete_preview_incomplete`: always-present inspection-completeness boolean; existing custom effects warning.
- `delete_preview_truncated`: detailed-preview-only boolean. True only when a finite cap omits another visible
  occurrence. Exact-at-cap, hidden-only tails and unlimited mode do not trigger it. No omitted-count API.
- `protected_objects`: selected ordered entries with exactly `obj` and `url=None`; absent with previews off.
- `related_objects`: selected cascade nodes with `obj`, optional `url`, `children`; absent with previews off.
- `related_summary` / `related_summary_items`: complete existing summary/redaction policy, independent of
  selected details; absent with previews off. Do not replace full counts with displayed counts.
- Run the effective core/Guardian/application visibility filter first; select protected-first then depth-first.
  Every displayed occurrence consumes a slot, including repeated objects, ancestors and the root. Omit hidden
  nodes and promote permitted children in order, finite or unlimited. URL work follows selection only.
- Selected solo-root details render even when summaries are empty. Root alone at limit one fills the cap;
  root plus a visible child overflows. Empty selected cascades omit their entire detail heading/list.

| `crud_views/snippets/delete/related_objects_tree.html` | Selected nodes only; no restricted placeholders. Existing optional cascade anchors and nested lists. |
| `crud_views/tags/alert.html` | Shared frame retains heading, icon, role and dismissal; custom leaf renders text/body as well as items. Do not send protected dictionaries through generic item normalization. |

Exact source message: **This preview is shortened. Additional objects are not shown.** It is independent of
**This preview is incomplete. Custom deletion behavior may affect additional objects.** Both may appear.

The migration retains exactly one adjacent Django fence for each marker:

- `<!-- protected-delete-recipe: content -->`
- `<!-- protected-delete-recipe: related -->`
- `<!-- protected-delete-recipe: body -->`

Consumers capture exact UTF-8/LF body bytes through the final newline, with no trim/dedent. Python and browser
loaders share this convention and SHA-256 identity. Body customization does not remove the need to migrate outer
guards/notices. Cascade anchors navigate to full pages from pages or modals; actual requests remain authoritative.

presentation. The display cap bounds selected occurrences, not total graph memory, filter cost or total queries;
custom labels/URL hooks can query arbitrarily. Unlimited mode retains all permitted presentation work.
