## Accomplishments

- Added the no-op `workflow_check_transition(self, name, *, user)` hook and independent private business/comment checks. Single/list Django errors retain order and interpolation; dictionary-shaped hook errors raise a clear TypeError.
- `WorkflowForm.clean()` returns its mapping, validates offered names with an owner, retains ownerless validation, and attaches simultaneous errors to their fields. Radio GET does not call the business hook.
- Added `run_transition(object, name, *, user, comment=None)` with lazy export and complete WorkflowInfo return. The engine rechecks availability before business validation or dispatch, normalizes comments, and explicitly binds object/content-type/history writes to the selected alias.
- Preserved the independent default change/request gates, `on_transition` inside the atomic boundary, and successful commit callback context. The configured alias selector runs once after scoped resolution.
- Added workflow-local catch-after-unwind recovery. It retains the original form and raw custom/comment input, fetches through the scoped queryset on the pinned alias, refreshes object-derived form choices, and rebuilds context without stale history. Page/modal failures return 200/422; ordinary execution ValidationError still propagates.
