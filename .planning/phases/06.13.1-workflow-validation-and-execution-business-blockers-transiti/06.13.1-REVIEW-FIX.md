### CR-01

Installed django-fsm source confirmed exact-source selection takes precedence
over `*`, then `+`, through its available-transition iterator. Regressions cover
stacked decorators with both REQUIRED/OPTIONAL policy orderings, helper and
radio/buttons POST paths, and current-state labels/help in NEW and ACTIVE states.
They also exercise exact-source precedence over a later wildcard and both wildcard
forms. Existing availability-before-business and execution permission checks stay
in place. Metadata now comes from the same current-user selection as form choices.

### CR-02

The view still calls the private engine and catches only its own refusal marker.
Consumers of the public helper receive ordinary `ValidationError`; business and
comment failures preserve expected messages. The regression checks sanitized
literal messages, codes, ordering and original exception cause. Nested helper
failures from transition, `on_transition`, and `cv_on_commit` propagate through
page/modal requests with either `cv_atomic` setting. Transition/on-transition
failures roll back primary state/history. Commit-callback failures leave primary
state/history committed and never run the invalid-form hook. Dependent state and
history remain unchanged in every case. Existing unrelated-exception identity and
rollback tests pass.

### CR-03

Guardian delegates the supplied queryset to the next loader before applying its
existing object permission and enabled-action gates. Real page/modal dispatch
regressions cover successful late-refusal redisplay, initial denial, permission
revocation, action disabling, and scoped 404. They check fresh object identity,
the supplied `default` alias, retained scope predicate and bound comment input,
unchanged state, and absent history. This does not claim new cross-database
Guardian support. Existing workflow alias and Guardian policy suites also pass.
