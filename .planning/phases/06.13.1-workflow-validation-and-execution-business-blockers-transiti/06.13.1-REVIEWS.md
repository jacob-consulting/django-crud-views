## Cross-Plan Summary & Database Contract Assessment

### Assessment of Revised Bounded Database Contract (D-14–D-17)
The revised database contract across Plans `06.13.1-01`, `06.13.1-02`, and `06.13.1-05` is well-matched to `06.13.1-CONTEXT.md` (D-14–D-17) and `CLAUDE.md`'s Developer Maintainability rule:
1. **Single selected alias:** Standalone `run_transition` selects `router.db_for_write(type(object), instance=object)`; `WorkflowView` defaults `cv_get_db_alias()` to `router.db_for_write(type(self.object), instance=self.object)` (matching `DeleteView.cv_get_db_alias` in `src/crud_views/lib/views/delete.py:147-148`) and pins `_cv_workflow_db_alias` once after resolving `self.object`.
2. **Pre-execution identity alignment:** Both paths require a persisted `object` and `user` whose `_state.db` equals the selected alias, raising `ImproperlyConfigured` before invoking the transition or business hook if unsaved or mismatched.
3. **Explicit same-alias binding without router introspection:** Inside `transaction.atomic(using=alias)`, the engine runs `object.save(using=alias)`, `ContentType.objects.db_manager(alias).get_for_model(object)`, and `WorkflowInfo.objects.using(alias).create(..., user_id=user.pk, workflow_object_content_type_id=content_type.pk)`, while `get_workflow_info_queryset()` (`src/crud_views_workflow/lib/mixins.py:62-72`) reads from `self._state.db`. Rejecting `router.routers` introspection avoids brittle coupling to custom router internals while ensuring that all workflow state and history operations execute atomically on the selected database.
