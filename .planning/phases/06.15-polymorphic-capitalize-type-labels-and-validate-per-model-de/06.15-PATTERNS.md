- **Permissions/navigation:** use existing permission CBV composition; no new authorization layer. Selector IDs/filtering/custom forms and cancellation stay on current paths.

- **State:** declarative class config is shared; selected groups remain local to view/request. Neither checks nor mapping selection discovers objects or subtypes.
- **Verification:** collect parent `tests/test1` with a focused `-k` per research to initialize Django settings; use multicore pytest for broad runs.
