## Private interface contract

- `ancestor_policy.py` holds execution-free `AncestorPermissions` and `AncestorPolicy`; settings may import it without ViewSet/Guardian.
- `ancestors.py` owns `_walk_ancestors`, static/operation selection, `_authorize_ancestors`, `_resolved_parent`, immediate-parent helper and relationship enforcement. A single successful guard stores request/view-local resolved objects. No long-lived user/object/access state.
- `ViewSet._ancestor_provider` supplies model/guardian class default; explicit policy overrides are validated. `_ancestor_has_permission(user, permission, obj, *, view)` is a private narrow provider seam, not a public plugin registry. Guardian implementation stays in its extension.
- Core/plain, core permission-required, Guardian and manage dispatch bridges call `_cv_ancestor_guard` before their remaining local work. Actor-only adapters never call state-loading local hooks. Existing dispatch/super behavior remains.
- `ancestor_db.py` validates actual reached alias/route/identity and throws `ImproperlyConfigured`; it does not select or replace a database. Optional Guardian route inventory remains extension-owned. Child scope and custom queryset consumers need proof at their real consumption seams.
