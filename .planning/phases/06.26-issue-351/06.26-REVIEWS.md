The main risks are the size of Task 1 and a few fixture details the plan states imprecisely.

**Strengths**

- **Admission change is minimal and correct.** `src/crud_views/lib/formsets/compact.py:84` is the exact tuple; lines 93–97 already enforce exact class, stock template and stock `input_type` for unmarked widgets.
