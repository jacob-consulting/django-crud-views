## Separately identified concrete finding: UI-F01

**Open — focus is lost after real modal content replacement.** On keyboard Enter opening, activeElement is
`#cv-modal`. Tab cycles Close → Confirm deletion → Delete → Cancel → Close. Submit the unchecked form with
keyboard Enter on Delete: the rendered crispy form already has `noValidate=true`, so it makes a real POST and
receives 422. Immediately after content replacement activeElement is **BODY**, `focusVisible=false`, outside
the modal. The next Tab reaches Close and resumes the cycle. After adding a native Contract through the fixture
host and submitting confirmed Delete, the blocked 422 likewise leaves focus on BODY; both notices remain and
the entire form is omitted. Close works by Enter; it too leaves BODY rather than restoring the trigger.

Source: `src/crud_views/static/crud_views/js/modal.js:43` assigns content.innerHTML, `:91` accepts 422 and
`:104` injects replacement without a focus target; `adapters.js` retains Bootstrap show/hide behavior. Both JS
files are byte-unchanged from pre-gap baseline `74c7f63` (`git diff 74c7f63 -- <both files>` is empty).
This is a newly observed condition, **not evidence that repair plans introduced it**; the baseline browser was
not separately run. Keyboard control functionality and next-Tab recovery do not establish preserved visible
focus at replacement. No unapproved focus-policy implementation was added. Independent review owns severity
and remediation; final acceptance remains open. Any newly required repair needs its own reviewed scope.
