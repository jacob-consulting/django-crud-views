## Interaction and Visibility Contract

Render the incomplete warning first inside `cv_form_body`, before the business-protection branch and outside
the form. Then retain the existing business-error versus related-preview branch. In the ordinary related branch,
show native protection before summary/tree. Both incomplete and native blocked notices may coexist. Business
protection retains its existing branch and independently omits the form; its warning must still be visible.

| Inspection / protection state | Notice and details | Confirmation |
|--------------------------------|--------------------|--------------|
| Complete, unblocked | Existing related content when present | One existing form |
| Incomplete, unblocked | Incomplete warning; available filtered details and partial heading | One existing form |
| Complete, native blocked | Existing visible-label or generic blocked notice | No form |
| Incomplete, native blocked | Incomplete warning plus visible-label or generic blocked notice | No form |
| Business protected, either completeness | Existing business error; incomplete warning when applicable | No form |
| Details disabled | No relationship keys/details; incomplete/native blocked notices still reflect their independent booleans | Determined by protection, never visibility |

Always expose `has_protected_objects` and `delete_preview_incomplete` independently. Only expose filtered related
context when `cv_show_related_objects=True`. Keep raw blockers, boundaries and alias names out of context.
Protected-label lists have no per-hidden-object placeholders. Preserve the existing cascade tree's `None`
redaction positions and `(restricted)` presentation; these are distinct from protected-label lists. Permitted
Unicode labels retain their text and Django escaping, in stock templates and native template overrides.
Keep aggregate disclosure even when labels are hidden; a partial heading makes no complete-effects promise.

GET renders the same content in page or modal without deletion callbacks. Invalid/blocked POST returns 200
for the full page and 422 with replacement content for the modal. Success retains 302 navigation or 204 with
`X-CV-Redirect`. HTTP DELETE remains 405. Missing/unauthorized targets retain existing denial responses and
cannot reveal preview data. Native late protection uses the approved friendly path; arbitrary callback errors
propagate. An incomplete preview does not promise that the eventual delete will succeed.

Keep native checkbox labels, keyboard operation, focus styling, existing modal adapter/dismiss controls and
translated Close labels. Alerts retain `role="alert"`; no extra live region or focus-stealing behavior is added.
