## Approved contract

- All default ordinary formset rows receive a card-style fieldset with a singular model-name legend, whether conditional or unconditional.
- `FormSet.row_legend=None` is the default and derives the row model's singular verbose name.
- A string or lazy translation supplies a custom row legend.
- `row_legend=False` removes the entire row fieldset, title, border, and card padding. Keep only the existing functional row wrapper and form contents. A border without a title was explicitly rejected.
- Each nested level is configured independently. The questionnaire example retains Question cards and renders Choice rows without frames.
- The formset section `title` remains independent. Compact formsets retain their existing presentation.
- Initial render, validation redisplay, and AJAX-added rows follow the same configuration.
