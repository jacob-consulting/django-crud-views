## Query budgets

Core native/conditional compact rendering uses **1/2 queries per row** and **20/40 for 20 rows**, independent of
2 versus 20 options, including all-no-fields guards. The projection adds one offered-options pass per row;
schema/helper inspection is query-free. Ordinary rendering and selected validation retain their dedicated
query-budget assertions. There is no scan of unused categories or cross-request cache.

Downstream warm native/conditional rendering uses **3/6 queries for 1 row** and **60/120 for 20 rows**, unchanged
for 2 versus 20 options. Each pass is one base-product query plus Beans and Equipment subtype fetches.
Labels/category reads add zero queries; native cleaning keeps its query count and subtype/annotation identity.
Cold ContentType lookup overhead is outside that warm comparison. Application-owned label/category properties
can add their own costs, as documented.
