**Related:** `src/crud_views_guardian/lib/mixins.py:416-420`; `src/crud_views/lib/view/base.py:95-107`

**Issue:** The added MRO-suffix check runs for both top-level and nested object/queryset compositions. GuardianParentPermissionMixin.has_permission() delegates to that suffix only at the top level. This is inaccurate diagnostics, not an authorization bypass.
