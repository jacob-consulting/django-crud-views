## Resource permission label and styling

The maintainer requested another label type after the assistant identified model/Guardian-specific
matrix wording. The agreed label is `resource`, denoting mapped Django permissions, not item grants.

An initial neutral-style suggestion was not approved. When the maintainer asked to settle styling,
the assistant proposed text `resource`, key `manage:badge-resource` and `badge bg-info text-dark`,
distinct from model/object badges and overridable through the registry.

**User's choice:** “approved”. The exact key and default classes are locked, not planning discretion.
