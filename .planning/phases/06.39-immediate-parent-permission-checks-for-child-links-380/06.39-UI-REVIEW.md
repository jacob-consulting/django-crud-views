### Experience Design

- **Own-first decision:** `src/crud_views/lib/view/base.py:528` preserves own-access denial and top-level
  delegation, then checks the supplied immediate parent. Context and card callers remain single-hook consumers
  at `src/crud_views/lib/view/buttons.py:242` and `src/crud_views/lib/views/card.py:63`.
- **Denied controls are inert:** `tests/test1/test_guardian_child_link_visibility.py:160` exercises both renderers
  and both hide/disable modes. The host GET succeeds while the child GET is 403; hidden output has no control,
  and disabled output is a native disabled button with no `href`, `data-cv-modal`, `onclick`, or `formaction`
  (`:185`–`:199`). These are inspected test assertions and reused passing evidence, not captured browser states.
- **Allowed destinations retain identity and empty collections:** the same regression grants Publisher view,
  refetches the actor, checks the exact parent URL and receives GET 200 with an empty collection (`:201`–`:208`).
  Permission denial is not inferred from the absence of children.
- **Own denial and Guardian preservation:** the own-denial test at `:214` asserts zero parent-helper calls.
  `src/crud_views_guardian/lib/mixins.py:297` preserves actor admission and the standalone queryset exception;
  `src/crud_views_guardian/lib/views.py:388` reuses create's existing parent-aware access check. The documented
  focused suite covers once-only calls, custom denial, provider policy, independent state, and ordinary/sibling
  preservation. No loading, async error, destructive-action, or new form workflow was introduced.
- **Approved limits remain visible:** farther ancestors may still deny a visible link's GET; invalid parent
  configuration can raise `ImproperlyConfigured` during host rendering. CONTEXT D-04/D-10 explicitly retain
  these limits, and reference/migration prose explains them. They are not unimplemented phase requirements.
- **Cost is disclosed:** COST records one parent-helper call, distinct from provider/cache-dependent SQL.
  The two-argument hook cannot reuse a request-local Guardian checker. This limitation is documented rather
  than hidden by a claim of universally zero extra queries.
