### Concerns
- **MEDIUM — The named classifier cannot separate checker grant SELECTs from other permission queries.**
  - The plan requires that distinction (`.planning/phases/06.49-reuse-guardian-object-grants-across-navigation-checks-385/06.49-01-PLAN.md:68`) but points the executor at `sql_categories` (`...06.49-01-PLAN.md:65`).

### Suggestions
- Use a small local classifier in the new test: a `permissions` query whose SQL joins `guardian_userobjectpermission` or `guardian_groupobjectpermission` (or an enabled direct-FK grant table) is a checker fill; the rest is model or vocabulary work.
