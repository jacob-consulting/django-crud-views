## Accomplishments

- Added `PropertyGroupConfig.id` with strict before-validation, appended `ResolvedGroup.id`, and preserved the token through `resolve_group`.

- Repeated real author requests use the documented `cv_is_modal_request(self.request)` / `X-CV-Modal: true` recipe to select `author-page` and `author-modal`. - The real-view test proves `get_object_for_detail()` returns the authorized `self.object` by identity and issues zero queries.
- `_render_scope` stores its registry on the first active non-base render frame. Includes, `include only`, inheritance and explicitly transported nested renders share the scope; direct tag operations, subsequent renders on the same Context/request, concurrent contexts and independent nested renders do not retain it.
