The no-install boundary is supported by unchanged dependency files and execution records. Exact cases and per-task runs are mapped in [VALIDATION](06.51-VALIDATION.md) and [ACCEPTANCE](06.51-ACCEPTANCE.md).

The authored register is complete and every threat is closed at configured L1 depth; the secure-phase workflow therefore takes its documented clean-register short-circuit. Browser/downstream, recorder-enabled, installed-distribution and alternate Python/Django lanes remain unverified.
