each marker occupies a whole line immediately before its opening django fence, with no blank line or prose
between them; the closing fence is on its own line. Capture the body after the opening fence's LF up to but
excluding the closing fence, preserving its final LF. Do not trim, dedent or normalize the capture before loading
or SHA-256 hashing. Require exactly one content, related and body marker and exactly one associated fence each;
duplicate, missing, separated or wrong-language markers are errors. All three consumers implement this same
contract and compare hashes, rather than accepting heading-based positional extraction in one consumer only.
