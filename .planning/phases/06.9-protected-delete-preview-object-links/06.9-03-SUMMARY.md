## Exact Recipe Handoff

Markers each occupy a whole line immediately before a `django` fence. Capture after the opening LF through the final body LF, excluding the closing fence; never trim, dedent or normalize. Reject duplicate/missing/separated/wrong-language markers. Hash UTF-8 bytes.

| Marker suffix | Template identity | Bytes | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---:|---|
| content | `myapp/delete.content.html` | 869 | `f82e8589112bc8345308aa2721a4f76b87c84b120301f55614d8a6f5dcac9716` |
| related | `myapp/delete_related.html` | 1234 | `9c10cde5fd9cb14119ae2ccc6433ac6c5796a6e5c8f9898413b2aacd9e44e6ce` |
| body | `myapp/delete_protected.html` | 223 | `9d350661866267cfbeae1194b88e92264425beeb07806db115cfd1362bd70762` |

The full marker is `<!-- protected-delete-recipe: NAME -->`. These are logical template identities loaded by test dictionaries, not repository files. `test_protected_recipe_marker_contract` verifies mutations and final-newline identity. Plan 04 must consume the same bytes and compare hashes.
